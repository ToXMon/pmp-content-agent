## Susanisms she explicitly identified

- **Stakeholder shorthand:** Anyone who might possibly care about the project, even if they do not yet know about it, is worth considering during stakeholder identification. She distinguishes this shorthand from the formal definition and notes that all team members are stakeholders, but not all stakeholders are team members. Transcript lines 2565–2591.
- **Find stakeholders through the network:** Start with the sponsor’s known contacts, invite them to an informal project conversation, and ask them to forward the invitation to others who may care. Then ask those identified about people outside the organization, such as regulators or other affected groups. Susan presents this as a practical experience-based technique. Transcript lines 2687–2725.

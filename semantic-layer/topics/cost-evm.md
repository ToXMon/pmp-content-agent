# Earned value, variances and cost forecasts

Topic ID: `COST-EVM`

## Related concepts and aliases

earned value management, earned value, planned value, actual cost, cost performance index, schedule performance index, estimate at completion, budget at completion.

Search aliases: EVM, EV, PV, AC, CPI, SPI, EAC, BAC, CV, SV.

## When it matters

Use this when you need to distinguish progress earned, progress planned and money spent at a reporting date.

## Practitioner guidance

At the same reporting date, compare EV with AC for cost and with PV for schedule. CV = EV − AC; SV = EV − PV; CPI = EV / AC; SPI = EV / PV, with nonzero denominators. State the performance assumption behind any estimate at completion.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor uses EV first as a mnemonic and emphasizes identifying which quantity or formula the scenario asks for.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

EVM needs a meaningful cost and progress measurement basis. Use it on baselined work where applicable; iteration metrics alone do not supply all EVM inputs.

## Related project artifacts

Performance measurement baseline, Cost forecast, Status report.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

SPI expresses schedule performance in earned-value terms. It does not tell you the delay in calendar days.

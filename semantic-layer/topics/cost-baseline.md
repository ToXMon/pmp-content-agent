# Cost estimation, budgeting and funding limits

Topic ID: `COST-BASELINE`

## Related concepts and aliases

cost baseline, contingency reserve, funding limit reconciliation, blended team rate, budget at completion.

Search aliases: cost budgeting, funding ceiling, cost estimates.

## When it matters

Use this when estimated costs need to fit funding available in particular periods.

## Practitioner guidance

Build estimates from the work or the team's expected cost. Check when funds are available, reconcile the spending plan against those limits, and distinguish risk contingency from ordinary estimates.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor calls out funding limit reconciliation and compares estimating by individual resource with using a blended team rate.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Predictive budgeting can aggregate activity costs. Adaptive planning may use a blended team rate. Hybrid can use both where appropriate.

## Related project artifacts

Cost baseline, Funding schedule, Cost estimates.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

An approved total budget does not mean the full amount is available to spend at any point in time.

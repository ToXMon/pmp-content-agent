# Schedule baseline and variance

Topic ID: `SCHEDULE-BASELINE`

## Related concepts and aliases

schedule baseline, baseline schedule, milestones, schedule deviation.

Search aliases: approved schedule, schedule slippage.

## When it matters

Use this when stakeholders need a reliable comparison between intended and actual dates.

## Practitioner guidance

Approve a schedule benchmark, collect actual progress, and investigate differences before proposing action. Communicate the forecast separately from the approved baseline and use formal control for baseline changes.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course emphasizes recognizing why and when a benchmark is approved, then distinguishing measurement from approval to revise it.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use a baseline on predictive commitments and fixed hybrid milestones. Adaptive teams may track a release forecast separately from governed dates.

## Related project artifacts

Schedule baseline, Project schedule, Performance report, Change request.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Updating the current schedule is not the same as replacing the approved baseline.

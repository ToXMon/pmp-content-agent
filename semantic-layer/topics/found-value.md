# Value, benefits and investment appraisal

Topic ID: `FOUND-VALUE`

## Related concepts and aliases

business value, cost-benefit analysis, net present value, opportunity cost, success measures.

Search aliases: CBA, NPV, investment appraisal.

## When it matters

Use this when choosing an investment or when stakeholders disagree about what success means.

## Practitioner guidance

Agree how stakeholders will measure outcomes. Compare costs with financial and nonfinancial benefits such as customer satisfaction. If comparing cash flows across periods, account for their timing and discount rate.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor uses cost-benefit analysis to illustrate why a nonfinancial benefit can belong in the assessment.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

A business case can justify a predictive project; incremental delivery gives an adaptive or hybrid project chances to check whether value is showing up.

## Related project artifacts

Business case, Benefits management plan, Success measures.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

A deliverable is an output. A benefit is an improvement expected from putting it to use. NPV is one appraisal technique, not a synonym for value.

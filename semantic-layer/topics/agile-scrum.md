# Scrum accountabilities and feedback events

Topic ID: `AGILE-SCRUM`

## Related concepts and aliases

Scrum, product owner, Scrum Master, developers, sprint planning, sprint review, sprint retrospective.

Search aliases: Scrum roles, Scrum events.

## When it matters

Use this when you need to know who orders work or which event to use for a feedback conversation.

## Practitioner guidance

Keep product ordering with the product owner and enable the team to organize its work. Use a review to inspect the increment with stakeholders and a retrospective to improve how the team works.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course discusses Scrum roles and events. Do not treat a course remark about who you are on the exam as a universal rule; use the named role in the scenario.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use Scrum practices for adaptive work in a hybrid project and agree how evidence from reviews reaches stakeholders outside the team.

## Related project artifacts

Product backlog, Sprint backlog, Increment, Improvement actions.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

The sprint review and sprint retrospective answer different questions about the product and how work happens.

# External environment and compliance change

Topic ID: `GOV-ENVIRONMENT`

## Related concepts and aliases

external business environment, PESTLE, regulatory change, environmental scanning.

Search aliases: macro environment, PESTEL, external factors.

## When it matters

Use this when a new law, economic change or other outside event may affect the project.

## Practitioner guidance

Scan for external changes and consult the people who track them. Add uncertain effects to risk monitoring. Assess a live impact and route it through the applicable governance, compliance or change process.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor flags tools for assessing the external environment. Separate monitoring a possible regulation from acting on one that applies now.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

All approaches need to respond to external constraints. In hybrid, identify whether a new requirement also affects a fixed commitment.

## Related project artifacts

Risk register, Issue log, Change request, Compliance records.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Market or regulatory scanning is different from approving the project's response.

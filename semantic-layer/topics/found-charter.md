# Business case, charter and project life cycle

Topic ID: `FOUND-CHARTER`

## Related concepts and aliases

business case, project charter, sponsor authorization, project life cycle.

Search aliases: project authorization, project mandate.

## When it matters

Before funding or starting a project, separate the case for investing from the authority to do the work.

## Practitioner guidance

Work with the sponsor to document the objective, constraints and authority needed to start. Use the business case to explain the investment; keep expected benefits visible as planning proceeds.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor flags the life cycle and charter as study material. Identify the artifact by its purpose rather than by its format.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use the charter to establish authority before using either a detailed predictive plan or an evolving backlog.

## Related project artifacts

Business case, Project charter, Benefits management plan.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

A business case justifies the investment. A charter authorizes the project. Neither is a detailed delivery plan.

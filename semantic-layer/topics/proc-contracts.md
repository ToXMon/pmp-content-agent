# Procurement and contract selection

Topic ID: `PROC-CONTRACTS`

## Related concepts and aliases

fixed-price contract, cost-reimbursable contract, time and materials, vendor selection, procurement negotiation.

Search aliases: T&M, supplier contracting, contract type.

## When it matters

Use this when acquiring external work or negotiating a change with a supplier.

## Practitioner guidance

Agree requirements and acceptance evidence with procurement. Compare contract types against how clearly work can be defined and where cost risk belongs. Check who may negotiate, approve and sign.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course compares contract types and describes the PM's input into negotiation. Read the scope certainty and approval authority in the scenario.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

A fixed price can fit well-defined work. Adaptive or hybrid arrangements still need a clear contract boundary and a way to handle changes.

## Related project artifacts

Procurement statement of work, Contract, Selection criteria.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Providing delivery input does not give the PM contract-signing authority.

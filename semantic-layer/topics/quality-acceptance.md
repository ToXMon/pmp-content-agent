# Acceptance criteria and definition of done

Topic ID: `QUALITY-ACCEPTANCE`

## Related concepts and aliases

acceptance criteria, definition of done, deliverable verification, increment quality.

Search aliases: DoD, done criteria.

## When it matters

Use this before work starts so people agree how to tell that a deliverable is acceptable and complete.

## Practitioner guidance

Write acceptance criteria for the item or deliverable. Agree which common quality checks the team applies before counting an increment as done. Confirm who accepts the deliverable when formal approval is required.

## PMP exam reasoning

Session 5 notes that teams may distinguish the terms and offers simplified exam advice. Treat that as instructor guidance; use the scenario and do not infer a guaranteed PMI equivalence.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Predictive work often uses deliverable acceptance criteria. Adaptive work can pair item-level criteria with a shared definition of done. Hybrid teams may also need contractual sign-off.

## Related project artifacts

Acceptance criteria, Definition of done, Test evidence, Acceptance record.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Meeting internal completion checks is different from obtaining formal stakeholder acceptance.

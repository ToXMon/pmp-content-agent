# Performance reports, status and forecasting

Topic ID: `MONITOR-STATUS`

## Related concepts and aliases

performance reporting, status report, forecast, variance, leading indicator.

Search aliases: project status, progress report, forecast completion.

## When it matters

Use this when a sponsor needs to know what changed and what is likely to happen next.

## Practitioner guidance

Collect current progress and compare with the relevant plan or baseline. State a forecast and its assumptions separately, explain material deviations and flag decisions needed.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course points out that forecasts need evidence across work areas, not just a schedule reading.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Predictive reports often compare against baselines. Adaptive reviews can supplement reporting with increments and flow indicators. Hybrid joins the two without equating their measures.

## Related project artifacts

Status report, Performance report, Forecast.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Status describes what has happened. A forecast estimates what may happen next.

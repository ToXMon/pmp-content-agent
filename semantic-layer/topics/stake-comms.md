# Communications planning and feedback

Topic ID: `STAKE-COMMS`

## Related concepts and aliases

communications management plan, communication model, encoding, decoding, noise, feedback.

Search aliases: communication plan, comms plan.

## When it matters

When people receive late or conflicting information, agree who needs which update, how and when.

## Practitioner guidance

Ask stakeholders what they need to know and how they prefer to receive it. Use a feedback loop to check understanding, especially when decisions or handoffs depend on a message.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor uses encode, decode and noise to illustrate a possible question design, not a confirmed exam item; the two-plan slide itself is not captured.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Formal reports and adaptive review conversations can coexist. Agree what each channel is for to avoid reporting the same information twice.

## Related project artifacts

Communications management plan, Stakeholder engagement plan, Status report.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Sending a message does not confirm understanding. Communications logistics differ from the work of gaining stakeholder support.

# Product backlog and adaptive scope

Topic ID: `SCOPE-BACKLOG`

## Related concepts and aliases

product backlog, backlog refinement, user story, prioritization, product owner.

Search aliases: backlog ordering, adaptive scope.

## When it matters

Use this when new stakeholder feedback competes with already planned work.

## Practitioner guidance

Capture the need and discuss its value and consequences with stakeholders. The product owner orders the backlog so the team knows which work to consider next.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course compares a predictive scope baseline to an evolving backlog. Identify who owns the ordering and whether the work actually changes a governed commitment.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Adaptive work uses backlog ordering and refinement. Hybrid can use a backlog within the parts of scope that may evolve, subject to contractual or baseline boundaries.

## Related project artifacts

Product backlog, User story, Acceptance criteria.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

A request entering the backlog does not mean it is committed for delivery. Backlog refinement is distinct from approving a baseline change.

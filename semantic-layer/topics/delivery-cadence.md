# Delivery cadence, pull and timeboxes

Topic ID: `DELIVERY-CADENCE`

## Related concepts and aliases

delivery cadence, timebox, on-demand delivery, pull, Kanban, work in progress.

Search aliases: WIP, continuous flow, timeboxed delivery.

## When it matters

Use this when choosing between working in fixed-length periods and pulling new work as capacity opens.

## Practitioner guidance

Agree how often stakeholders need usable work or feedback. For flow, make active work visible and avoid starting more than the team can handle. For timeboxed work, use the iteration boundary to plan and inspect progress.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor flags the comparison between on-demand and timeboxed delivery. Identify whether work moves by capacity or a fixed cadence.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Adaptive teams can use flow or timeboxes; hybrid teams should coordinate their cadence with any fixed milestones or release approvals.

## Related project artifacts

Kanban board, Product backlog, Release schedule.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

A WIP limit constrains active work. It is not a forecast of how much work will be completed.

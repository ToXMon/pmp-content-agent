# Procurement and contract closeout

Topic ID: `PROC-CLOSE`

## Related concepts and aliases

contract closeout, vendor deliverables, procurement acceptance, contract records.

Search aliases: close procurement, supplier closeout.

## When it matters

Use this after a supplier delivers, before treating its contract as closed.

## Practitioner guidance

Check deliverables and acceptance against the agreement. Work with procurement to resolve outstanding matters, confirm the required closeout actions and retain the records.

## PMP exam reasoning

Session 5 asks what remains before formal contract closure when some steps have been done already.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use the applicable contract closeout process regardless of how the team delivered the supplier work.

## Related project artifacts

Contract, Acceptance evidence, Procurement records.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Shipping everything is not proof of acceptance or administrative closure. Closing a contract is separate from closing the whole project.

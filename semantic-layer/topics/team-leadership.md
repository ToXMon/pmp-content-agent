# Servant leadership and team empowerment

Topic ID: `TEAM-LEADERSHIP`

## Related concepts and aliases

servant leadership, team empowerment, delegation, emotional intelligence, motivation.

Search aliases: servant leader, self-managing team.

## When it matters

Use this when a team waits for permission on decisions it could own or when performance problems point to unmet needs.

## Practitioner guidance

Ask what is preventing the team from doing its work. Remove barriers, delegate within clear boundaries, and tailor your support to the team rather than taking over its decisions.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course calls out autonomy and motivation. In a scenario, examine what support the team needs before adding control.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Adaptive teams emphasize self-management. Predictive and hybrid teams can also delegate decisions while keeping organizational accountability clear.

## Related project artifacts

Team charter, Roles and responsibilities, Impediment log.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Empowerment needs decision boundaries. It is different from leaving a team without support.

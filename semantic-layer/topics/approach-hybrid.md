# Hybrid delivery and fixed commitments

Topic ID: `APPROACH-HYBRID`

## Related concepts and aliases

hybrid delivery, fixed contractual milestones, evolving requirements, blended planning.

Search aliases: hybrid project, blended approach, fixed dates and flexible scope.

## When it matters

Use hybrid when part of the work needs fixed commitments and another part needs customer feedback.

## Practitioner guidance

Identify which milestones or scope are committed and what can evolve in the backlog. Agree how the team will report progress and who can approve changes that cross the boundary.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course discusses a case with fixed contractual milestones and evolving requirements. Explain why both conditions matter before choosing hybrid.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use predictive controls on committed work and adaptive planning where requirements evolve; coordinate milestones across both.

## Related project artifacts

Contract milestones, Product backlog, Schedule baseline, Status report.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Calling a project hybrid does not make a contractual date optional or put every backlog change through a board.

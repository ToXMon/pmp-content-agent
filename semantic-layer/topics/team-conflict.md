# Conflict management and resolution

Topic ID: `TEAM-CONFLICT`

## Related concepts and aliases

conflict resolution, collaboration, conflict escalation, Leas levels of conflict.

Search aliases: manage disagreements, team conflict, resolve conflict.

## When it matters

Use this when a disagreement begins to disrupt team decisions or relationships.

## Practitioner guidance

Find out what the parties disagree about and how severe it is. Help them acknowledge the problem and look for a resolution. Escalate when the severity or decision authority warrants it.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor warns against ignoring conflict and uses Leas' levels as a study aid. Read the severity and context before selecting a response.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use team working agreements and direct conversation across approaches. A retrospective can surface patterns, but active conflict may need attention before the next retro.

## Related project artifacts

Team charter, Issue log, Working agreements.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Collaboration is a way to work toward a resolution; escalation changes who must act or decide.

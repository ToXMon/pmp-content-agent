# Quality management and cost of quality

Topic ID: `QUALITY-MANAGE`

## Related concepts and aliases

quality management, cost of quality, prevention, appraisal, failure costs, continuous improvement.

Search aliases: COQ, quality planning.

## When it matters

Use this when deciding where to spend effort to prevent defects or how to address repeated quality problems.

## Practitioner guidance

Agree how quality will be measured. Compare the cost of preventive work and appraisal with the cost of failures and rework. Use recurring defects as input to improving the process.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course calls out cost of quality and compares quality responsibilities across approaches. Classify the activity before deciding which category of cost it belongs to.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

A predictive plan can specify checks and responsibilities. In adaptive work, quality checks can be built into the team's definition of done. Hybrid teams may add separate contractual acceptance.

## Related project artifacts

Quality management plan, Quality metrics, Test results, Definition of done.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Inspection finds defects; preventive work aims to keep them from occurring. Quality management is broader than obtaining sign-off.

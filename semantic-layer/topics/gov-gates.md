# Governance, phase gates and escalation

Topic ID: `GOV-GATES`

## Related concepts and aliases

governance framework, phase gate, decision authority, escalation, PMO.

Search aliases: go no-go review, stage gate, project management office.

## When it matters

When moving between phases or making a decision beyond the team's authority, find out who can decide and what evidence they need.

## Practitioner guidance

Agree escalation thresholds and review points early. Take status, options and implications to the responsible decision maker. Use organizational policies and templates where they apply.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor highlights phase boundaries and asks learners to distinguish what happens at a phase gate from regular reporting.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Predictive work may use formal phase gates. Adaptive work still follows governance and escalates decisions that exceed team authority; hybrid can combine iteration feedback with periodic governance review.

## Related project artifacts

Governance framework, Decision log, Phase review, Status report.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Visibility alone does not authorize an action. Escalation seeks a decision when it exceeds delegated authority.

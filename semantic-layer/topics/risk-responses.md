# Risk analysis and threat or opportunity responses

Topic ID: `RISK-RESPONSES`

## Related concepts and aliases

risk register, probability and impact matrix, risk response, threat, opportunity, risk owner, trigger.

Search aliases: risk assessment, risk mitigation, positive risk, negative risk.

## When it matters

Use this while an uncertain event could affect your objectives and there is time to prepare a response.

## Practitioner guidance

Record the uncertainty, rate probability and impact, then focus further analysis on decisions that justify it. Agree a response, owner and trigger. For threats consider mitigation or avoidance; for opportunities consider enhancing or exploiting them.

## PMP exam reasoning

Classify the uncertainty before selecting a response. A tactic that reduces a threat is not the same as one that increases a potential benefit.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Review risks as plans or backlogs change. Hybrid teams should include risks at the interface between fixed commitments and evolving work.

## Related project artifacts

Risk register, Probability-impact matrix, Risk response plan.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Risk mitigation is one response, not the name for all responses. A risk can be a threat or an opportunity.

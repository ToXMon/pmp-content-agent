# Risks, issues and impediments

Topic ID: `RISK-ISSUES`

## Related concepts and aliases

issue log, impediment, obstacle, risk trigger, issue owner.

Search aliases: impediment log, blocker, track issues.

## When it matters

Use this when something has already happened or a team is actively blocked.

## Practitioner guidance

Check whether the risk register already names a response or owner. Log the actual issue, assign a next action and review date, and escalate when the team cannot resolve it within its authority.

## PMP exam reasoning

Session 5 calls out the distinction: uncertainty about a future event belongs to risk reasoning; a problem already present needs issue action.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Predictive teams may use an issue log. Adaptive teams often track impediments where the team sees them; hybrid uses agreed escalation routes across both.

## Related project artifacts

Risk register, Issue log, Impediment log.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

An issue can create new risks. That does not make the issue itself uncertain again.

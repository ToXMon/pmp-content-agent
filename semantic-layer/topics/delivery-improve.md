# Retrospectives, lessons learned and improvement

Topic ID: `DELIVERY-IMPROVE`

## Related concepts and aliases

retrospective, lessons learned register, knowledge transfer, continuous improvement.

Search aliases: retro, lessons learned.

## When it matters

Use this after a pattern of problems, at an iteration boundary, or while preparing to transfer the work.

## Practitioner guidance

Make time to ask what worked and what should change. Record the learning and turn a useful improvement into assigned work; check later whether it helped.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course highlights how to prepare and facilitate a retrospective and recommends learning from resolved blockers.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use regular retrospectives in adaptive work; phase reviews and ongoing lessons capture can serve predictive work. Hybrid can use both.

## Related project artifacts

Lessons learned register, Improvement backlog, Action log.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

A lessons log preserves observations. Improvement still requires someone to act on them.

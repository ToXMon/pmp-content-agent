# Predictive and adaptive delivery selection

Topic ID: `APPROACH-TAILOR`

## Related concepts and aliases

predictive, adaptive, tailoring, requirements stability, uncertainty.

Search aliases: plan-based, plan-driven, change-based, delivery approach selection.

## When it matters

Pick a way of working before committing stakeholders to the wrong kind of plan.

## Practitioner guidance

Use requirements stability and the need for feedback to choose how much planning to do up front. Revisit the choice when constraints or uncertainty change.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor flags the comparison among predictive, adaptive and hybrid as study material. Read the scenario for constraints and uncertainty before picking an approach.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Predictive uses more up-front planning; adaptive refines work through feedback. Hybrid can mix the two where project conditions differ.

## Related project artifacts

Project management plan, Product backlog, Release plan.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Adaptive planning still happens. Choosing an approach does not remove the need to plan or manage change.

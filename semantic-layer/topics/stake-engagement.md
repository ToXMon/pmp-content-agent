# Stakeholder identification and engagement

Topic ID: `STAKE-ENGAGEMENT`

## Related concepts and aliases

stakeholder register, stakeholder analysis, stakeholder engagement assessment matrix, current and desired engagement.

Search aliases: stakeholder mapping, SEAM.

## When it matters

Use this when starting a project, when someone new is affected or when support drops.

## Practitioner guidance

Start with the sponsor's contacts and ask who else might be affected, including outside the organization. Record stakeholders and compare their current engagement with what the project needs. Revisit the plan as relationships change.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor separates stakeholder engagement from communications planning and points to a comparison slide. The transcript does not capture the slide's full contents.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use planned engagement reviews in predictive work and feedback opportunities in adaptive work. In hybrid, keep one view of who needs engagement across the workstreams.

## Related project artifacts

Stakeholder register, Stakeholder engagement assessment matrix, Stakeholder engagement plan.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

The register identifies stakeholders; an engagement plan describes how to work with them. Team members are stakeholders, but not every stakeholder is on the team.

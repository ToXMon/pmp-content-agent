# PMP situational reasoning and study practice

Topic ID: `EXAM-REASONING`

## Related concepts and aliases

scenario reasoning, first action, exam content outline, instructor callout, practice exams.

Search aliases: PMP mindset, what should the PM do first, ECO.

## When it matters

Use this when reading a study scenario that asks what the PM should do first or next.

## Practitioner guidance

Identify the delivery approach, what has already happened and who can decide. Where the facts are unclear, gather context and consult relevant people before choosing action or escalation. Follow mandatory rules when they apply.

## PMP exam reasoning

Study the applicable Exam Content Outline and practice timed scenarios. Treat key-slide labels and Susanisms as study cues, not guaranteed exam items or universal rules.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use context: baseline control and backlog refinement can each be appropriate for different scenarios. Avoid treating a memorized slogan as a substitute for the facts.

## Related project artifacts

Exam Content Outline, Practice review notes, Acronym list.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

A first-step heuristic is not a rule that overrides governance, ethical obligations or immediate needs.

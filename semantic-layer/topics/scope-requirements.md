# Requirements elicitation and traceability

Topic ID: `SCOPE-REQUIREMENTS`

## Related concepts and aliases

requirements elicitation, requirements traceability matrix, requirements validation, workshops.

Search aliases: RTM, requirements gathering, trace requirements.

## When it matters

Use this when requirements are unclear or delivery teams cannot tell where a requirement came from.

## Practitioner guidance

Choose elicitation techniques for the stakeholders and question: interviews for individual detail, facilitated workshops for shared decisions, or observation to see the work. Record requirement sources and trace changes to downstream work.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course reviews several elicitation techniques. Choose one based on the information and people in the scenario.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Predictive teams can maintain an explicit traceability matrix. Adaptive teams refine backlog items with stakeholders. Hybrid teams can keep traceability for commitments while refining implementation details.

## Related project artifacts

Requirements traceability matrix, Scope statement, Product backlog.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

A list of requirements does not show their origin, changes or verification unless it records those links.

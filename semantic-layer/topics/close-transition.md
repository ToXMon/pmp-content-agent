# Transition to operations and benefits sustainment

Topic ID: `CLOSE-TRANSITION`

## Related concepts and aliases

operational handover, transition support, benefits realization, sustainment, operational ownership.

Search aliases: BAU handover, transition to operations, benefit owner.

## When it matters

Use this when a team is preparing to hand over a deliverable or check whether expected benefits persist.

## Practitioner guidance

Plan who will own the result in operations and what training, documentation or support they need. Agree how outcomes will be tracked after project delivery.

## PMP exam reasoning

The course distinguishes delivering the result from transitioning and sustaining its benefits. Identify who can still act after the project team leaves.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

A predictive project can plan a final handover; an adaptive or hybrid project can arrange transition and feedback around releases.

## Related project artifacts

Transition plan, Benefits management plan, Handover checklist.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Project acceptance marks a decision about delivery. Benefits may be measured after closure by the receiving organization.

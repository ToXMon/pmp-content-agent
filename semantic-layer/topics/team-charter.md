# Team charter, norms and accountability

Topic ID: `TEAM-CHARTER`

## Related concepts and aliases

team charter, team norms, roles and responsibilities, Tuckman stages, team accountability.

Search aliases: working agreements, ground rules, forming storming norming performing.

## When it matters

Use this for a new team, after a membership change or when role expectations keep causing friction.

## Practitioner guidance

Create the working agreements with the team. Make responsibilities and behavioral expectations explicit; revisit the agreement when practice exposes a gap.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor highlights team development and expectations. Use the team's behavior to reason about its stage instead of memorizing labels alone.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Use a shared set of working agreements across approaches; self-managing teams can agree how to work without the PM prescribing every decision.

## Related project artifacts

Team charter, Roles and responsibilities, Working agreements.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

The project charter authorizes the project; a team charter records how people will work together.

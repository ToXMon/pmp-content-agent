# Schedule compression: fast tracking and crashing

Topic ID: `SCHEDULE-COMPRESSION`

## Related concepts and aliases

schedule compression, fast tracking, crashing, critical path.

Search aliases: overlap activities, add resources to shorten schedule.

## When it matters

Use this when the finish date needs to move earlier and removing scope is not an option under consideration.

## Practitioner guidance

Compare overlapping critical activities with adding resources to shorten them. Assess the rework risk or extra cost, check approval authority and recalculate the critical path after the change.

## PMP exam reasoning

The instructor stresses that compressing work away from the critical path will not advance the overall finish unless the schedule logic changes.

## Predictive, adaptive and hybrid relevance

Apply to dependency-based scheduling in predictive or hybrid work. In adaptive work, reconsider what can fit in the release instead of assuming overlap improves delivery.

## Related project artifacts

Project schedule, Network diagram, Change request.

## Common mistakes and distinctions

Fast tracking overlaps work; crashing adds resources, usually at a cost. Neither is the same as dropping scope.
